\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Symmetric robots walk further than unconstrained robots with the same number of hinges, and the lead builds as their controllers learn. Mirroring is not required for it: unmirrored bodies with the same resting shape match symmetric bodies, and so do bodies that duplicate modules without mirroring them. Symmetric bodies do lock their limbs in phase, and locked gaits score higher, but trained gaits depend no more on that timing than on any other. Comparisons of body encodings should therefore control for the bodies an encoding produces before crediting the regularity itself.
